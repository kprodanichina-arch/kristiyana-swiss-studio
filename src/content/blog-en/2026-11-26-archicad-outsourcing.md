**Archicad outsourcing** means handing part of your project work – modelling, construction documentation, details or visualisation – to an external team that works directly in Archicad. This article explains which tasks are worth outsourcing, how the handover works in practice (PLN files, BIMcloud guest access or IFC), what to agree before the first task and which mistakes cost the most time.

Search for the term and you mostly find freelance marketplaces and large offshore drafting firms. Both can work, but neither tells you how to set up the collaboration so that the model that comes back actually fits your office standard. That is what this guide is about.

## Which Archicad tasks can you outsource?

The best candidates are tasks with a clear input, a clear output and limited design decisions:

- **Construction documentation and working drawings** once the design is fixed – for example in RIBA Stage 4, HOAI LPH 5 or SIA phase 51/52.
- **Detail drawings** for façades, roofs, windows and junctions, based on your sketches or reference details.
- **Existing-building models** from point clouds, old drawings or survey data (scan to BIM).
- **Model clean-up and IFC preparation** before coordination with structural and MEP engineers.
- **Visualisations** from your Archicad model for competitions or client presentations.

What you should keep in-house: design decisions, client communication and anything where the brief is still moving every day. Outsourcing works when the external team receives decisions, not open questions.

## How does the handover work: PLN, BIMcloud or IFC?

There are three common ways to share an Archicad project with an external team. The right one depends on how closely you need to work together.

1. **PLN file exchange.** You send a project file (or a defined part of it), the external team works on it and sends it back. Simple and licence-independent, but only one side should edit at a time. Agree who "owns" the file at each moment and use clear file names with dates.
2. **Teamwork via BIMcloud.** Both teams work in the same project at the same time and reserve elements as needed. With BIMcloud SaaS on a cloud licence, company admins can invite users from other companies as **guests**. According to Graphisoft, guest users need a *portable* licence from their own company account – they cannot use your licences – and a portable licence can be used on only one BIMcloud at a time. Check with your Graphisoft partner whether your BIMcloud setup supports guest access before you plan around it.
3. **IFC exchange.** If the external team only delivers a reference model or works in other software, IFC is the neutral format. It is ideal for coordination, but less suitable if the result has to become a native, editable part of your Archicad model.

For most outsourced documentation work, Teamwork or a well-organised PLN exchange is the practical choice. IFC is the right choice when the outsourced work feeds coordination rather than your drawing set.

## What should you agree before the first task?

Most problems in Archicad outsourcing come from missing agreements, not from a lack of skill. Before work starts, clarify:

- **Scope and deliverables:** which floors, drawings or details, at what scale and level of detail, and in which phase.
- **Office template:** layers, layer combinations, pen sets, surfaces, building materials, classifications and properties. Ideally, the external team works directly in your template.
- **Library:** which library parts and custom objects are allowed; how missing objects are handled.
- **Views and layouts:** whether the external team also places drawings on layouts, or only delivers views.
- **Project origin and survey point:** essential if IFC models are exchanged with consultants later.
- **Review points:** when you check intermediate results – for example after the first floor or the first three details.
- **Data protection and confidentiality:** an NDA if needed, and which documents may be stored where.

A short written brief covering these points saves several rounds of corrections.

## Typical mistakes – and how to avoid them

- **Starting with the biggest package.** Test the collaboration with a small, clearly defined task first.
- **No template, no standards.** If the external team builds its own structure, merging the work back costs more than it saved.
- **Open design questions in the brief.** Every unresolved decision becomes a delay or a guess.
- **No single contact person.** One responsible person on each side keeps questions and answers in one place.
- **Checking only at the end.** A quick review of the first delivered sheet catches systematic errors early.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Archicad outsourcing only worth it for large projects?

No. Smaller practices often benefit most, because one large project can block the whole team. A clearly defined package – such as the details of one façade – is a good unit to outsource.

### Do we need extra Archicad licences for an external team?

Not for a PLN or IFC exchange: the external team works with its own licences. For BIMcloud guest access, Graphisoft states that guests need a portable licence from their own company account.

### How do we keep quality consistent?

By working in your office template, agreeing review points and checking the first delivery in detail. After that, spot checks are usually enough.
